# Review rubric

## Scores

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance | Could be sent to anyone | Right role, generic situation | Clearly about this person's situation or trigger |
| Clarity | Jargon, unclear what is offered | Understandable after two reads | Obvious in one read, plain words |
| Proof | None, or "leading companies" | A vague claim or unnamed customer | Named peer or precise number, approved |
| Ask | 30-60 minute meeting with a stranger, multiple asks | Meeting ask with soft framing | One easy, interest-based question |
| Brevity | 200+ words, paragraphs | 100-150 words | Under 90 words, each line earns its place |
| Deliverability | Links, images, spam words, attachments | One link or minor risk | Plain text, no links, no risky words |

Verdict: 25-30 send, 18-24 fix the top issues first, under 18 rewrite.

## Common lines and fixes

| Line | Why it hurts | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I hope this email finds you well." | Wasted first line; signals template | Delete; start with the observation |
| "My name is X and I work at Y." | About you; they can see your name | Delete |
| "We are the leading AI-powered platform..." | Unprovable claim, category jargon | State the outcome for them |
| "I wanted to reach out because..." | Filler | Start with the reason itself |
| "Would you be open to a 30-minute call next week?" | High commitment from a stranger | "Worth a look?" |
| "Let me know if you have any questions." | Passive, no question to answer | One specific question |
| "Just following up on my previous email." | Adds nothing | A new angle or value |

## Spam-risk phrases to remove

Free, guarantee, risk-free, act now, limited time, click here, 100%, $$$, urgent, winner, "dear friend", all caps words, three or more exclamation marks.

## Reading test

Read the email in under 15 seconds. If you cannot say what they offer and what they want from you, clarity is 2 or below.
