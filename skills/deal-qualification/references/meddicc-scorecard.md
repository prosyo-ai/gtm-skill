# MEDDICC scorecard

## Evidence examples

| Element | 1 (assumed) | 2 (partial) | 3 (confirmed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metrics | "They want to save time" | "Close takes 9 days, they'd like it faster" | "They target a 5-day close by Q3; each day saved is worth ~$40k" (stated by buyer) |
| Economic buyer | "Probably the CFO" | Champion named the CFO as approver | Met the CFO; she confirmed this is a Q3 priority |
| Decision criteria | Guessing from the RFP | Champion listed criteria verbally | Written criteria, with our differentiators included |
| Decision process | "They'll decide next month" | Steps known, no dates | Steps, people, dates agreed in a mutual plan |
| Paper process | Unknown | "Legal will need to look" | Security review 2 weeks, legal 1 week, PO process known |
| Identify pain | Generic pain | Pain described, impact vague | Pain + impact + reason for now in their words |
| Champion | Friendly contact | Shares information when asked | Took action: booked the EB meeting, shared internal docs |
| Competition | Unknown | "Looking at a couple of options" | Named alternatives, their view of each, why we lead |

## Stage minimums

| Stage | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery complete | Pain 2, Metrics 1, Champion 1 |
| Evaluation | Pain 3, Metrics 2, Champion 2, Decision criteria 2 |
| Proposal | All 2+, Economic buyer 2+ |
| Commit | All 2+, Pain 3, Champion 3, Economic buyer 3, Paper process 2+ |

## Champion test

A real champion has done at least two of these:
- Gave access to the economic buyer or other stakeholders.
- Shared internal information (priorities, budget timing, competitors).
- Sold the solution in a meeting you were not in.
- Pushed back on their own team on your behalf.
- Spent their own time on the evaluation (prep, data, trials).

A contact who likes you but has done none of these is a coach, not a champion.

## Red flags that override the score

- Close date moved twice without a new reason.
- No contact with anyone but the champion in 30 days.
- "Send me your best price" before any evaluation.
- Procurement involved before the economic buyer.
