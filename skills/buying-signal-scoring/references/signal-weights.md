# Signal weights and stacking

## Starter weights

Calibrate after one quarter by comparing which signals preceded real opportunities.

| Signal | Weight | Half-life | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demo or pricing form started but not finished | 40 | 3 days | Highest intent; human follow-up same day |
| Pricing or comparison page visits (company-level) | 30 | 7 days | Use as timing only, never mention it |
| Former champion joined a new company | 35 | 60 days | Strongest warm signal in B2B |
| New leader in buying role | 30 | 45 days | |
| Hiring for a role the product supports | 25 | Until closed | Two or more listings: +10 |
| Funding round | 20 | 60 days | Larger rounds raise weight only if the plan names your function |
| Engaged with your post or content | 15 | 14 days | Comment > like |
| Third-party category intent | 15 | 14 days | Noisy; treat as tie-breaker |
| Technology added or removed | 15 | 60 days | Strong if it is a complement or a competitor |
| Event attendance or speaking | 10 | 14 days | |
| Expansion news: office, region, product | 15 | 60 days | |
| Negative news: layoffs, bad quarter | 10 | 30 days | Changes the angle to efficiency |

Decay: weight × 0.5^(days since signal ÷ half-life).

## Stacking rules

- Same signal type twice in a week counts once.
- Two different signal types in 30 days: add a 1.2x bonus.
- Three or more types in 30 days: 1.5x bonus and a Tier 1 treatment regardless of the account's static tier.

## Play map

| Signal | Who | Angle | Channel | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New leader | The leader | First-90-days priorities | LinkedIn then email | Days 14-45 in seat |
| Hiring wave | Hiring manager | Help the new team ramp | Email | Within 7 days of the listing |
| Funding | Founder or functional VP | The growth plan's bottleneck | Email | Weeks 2-6 after news |
| Former champion moved | The champion | Reconnect, no pitch | LinkedIn message | Days 30-60 in new job |
| High-intent page visits | Likely buyer at the account | Helpful resource for the problem | Email | Within 24 hours |
| Content engagement | The person | Continue the conversation | LinkedIn | Within 48 hours |
