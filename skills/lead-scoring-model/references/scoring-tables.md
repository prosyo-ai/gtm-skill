# Scoring tables

## Lift method

1. Base rate: share of all leads that became an opportunity. Example: 4%.
2. For each attribute or action, its opportunity rate. Example: leads who visited pricing, 14%.
3. Lift = rate ÷ base. Pricing visit lift = 3.5.
4. Points ≈ 10 × log2(lift), rounded. Lift 2 → 10 points, lift 4 → 20, lift 8 → 30. Lift below 1.2 → 0. Lift below 0.8 → negative.

Only trust lifts computed on 30+ leads with that attribute.

## Thresholds

Start with fit ≥ 60 and engagement ≥ 40 for sales handoff, then tune weekly in the first month until the volume matches capacity.
