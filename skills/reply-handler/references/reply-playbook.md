# Reply playbook

## Interested

> Great, thanks Maya. The call is 20 minutes: how your close runs today, what Contoso changed, and whether it would work for you. Does Tuesday at 10 or Wednesday at 2 (your time) work? Or grab any slot here: <booking link>

## Question: price

> Fair question. It depends on the number of entities; most teams your size land between <range from the approved price list>. The 20-minute call is the fastest way to get you an exact number. Tuesday at 10 or Wednesday at 2?

If pricing is public, link to it and give the likely plan in one line.

## Question: how does it work / integrations

Answer in two or three sentences, plainly. Then: "Easier to show than tell. 15 minutes this week?"

## Referral

To the referrer:
> Thanks Tom, appreciated. I'll reach out to Priya and mention you pointed me her way.

To the referred person:
> Hi Priya, Tom suggested I get in touch about month-end close. <one-line problem + proof>. Worth 20 minutes?

## Objection: "We already use something"

> Makes sense. Out of curiosity, how long does close take with it today? If it's under 5 days, you're ahead of most and I'll leave you be.

## Objection: "No budget"

> Understood. When does planning for next year start? Happy to send something useful before then and not chase you in the meantime.

## Not now

> No problem. I'll check back in <month they named>. Anything specific you'd want to see by then?

Then: schedule the follow-up for that date, set the status to nurture.

## Not interested

> Understood, thanks for letting me know. I won't follow up.

Close the record with the reason if given.

## Wrong person

> Thanks for letting me know. Who would be best to talk to about <topic>? Happy to keep your name out of it if you prefer.

## Out of office

No reply. Pause all steps; resume two business days after the return date. If the auto-reply names a delegate, treat as a referral (without naming the out-of-office person as a "recommendation").

## Unsubscribe or hostile

No sales reply. Optional single line: "Done, you won't hear from us again." Suppress on email, domain-level for the person, and LinkedIn.
