# Persona examples

## Champion: Head of Sales Development (product: outbound automation)

```
Role: Head of SDR, SDR Manager, Director of Sales Development
Type: champion
Measured on: meetings held per rep, pipeline created, ramp time
Goals this year: hit meeting targets with a flat headcount; ramp new reps in under 60 days
Pains (their words):
  - "My reps spend half the day building lists instead of talking to people." (call notes)
  - "Every new rep writes their own sequences and half of them are bad." (call notes)
  - "We burned two domains last quarter." (community thread)
Triggers: new quarterly target, a rep resignation, a domain landing in spam, new VP Sales
Objections → proof:
  - "AI emails sound robotic" → side-by-side of a sourced, personalised draft
  - "My reps will resist it" → rep-level time saved per week from a similar team
  - "We already have a sequencer" → what it does not do: research and reply drafting
Trusted sources: sales development communities, peer SDR leaders, practitioner newsletters
Angle: more held meetings per rep without adding headcount
Do not say: "replace your SDRs", "10x", "set it and forget it"
```

## Blocker: Head of IT or Security (any SaaS that touches customer data)

```
Role: IT Manager, Head of Security, CISO at larger accounts
Type: blocker
Measured on: incidents, audit findings, vendor count, access reviews passed
Goals this year: pass the next audit; reduce shadow IT
Pains (their words):
  - "Sales buys tools and tells us after the contract is signed."
  - "I need to know where the data goes and who can see it."
Triggers: audit cycle, a vendor breach in the news, a new compliance requirement
Objections → proof:
  - "Where is data stored?" → data-flow diagram and sub-processor list
  - "Do you train on our data?" → written data-use policy
  - "SSO and access control?" → admin screenshots and role list
Trusted sources: security questionnaires, peer CISOs, compliance frameworks
Angle: a vendor review that takes one meeting, not six weeks
Do not say: "it is totally secure", "nobody has ever asked"
```

## Common do-not-say phrases by role

| Role | Tunes out at |
| --- | --- |
| Founders and CEOs | "Quick question", "I'd love to pick your brain", long intros about you |
| VP Sales | "Increase productivity" without a number, "AI-powered" as the lead |
| RevOps | "Seamless integration" without naming the objects and fields |
| Finance | "Pays for itself" without the maths |
| Marketing leaders | "Generate more leads" (they are measured on pipeline, not leads) |
| Engineers and IT | Superlatives of any kind |
