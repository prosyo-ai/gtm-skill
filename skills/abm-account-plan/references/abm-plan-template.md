# ABM one-page account plan

```
Account: <name>                Tier: 1:1 / 1:few / 1:many       Owners: <AE> + <marketer>
Why this account: <fit + potential value>
Current status: unaware / engaged / opportunity / customer

INSIGHT
What's happening: <strategy, triggers, initiatives, with sources>
Value hypothesis: Because <insight>, <account> could <outcome> by <how>, worth ~<impact>.

BUYING GROUP
Name | Title | Role (EB/champion/evaluator/user/blocker) | Status | Angle

PLAYS (next 60 days)
Week | Sales action | Marketing action | Target person | Goal

ASSETS
Custom insight piece / business case draft / reference customer / event invite

METRICS
Engaged contacts: target N by <date>
Meetings with buying group: target N
Opportunity: by <date>, ~<amount>

RISKS
<incumbent, budget timing, blocker, competing initiative>
```

## Example insight and hypothesis

Account: a national 3PL with 40 warehouses.

- What's happening: opened 6 new warehouses this year (press releases), hiring 12 operations supervisors (careers page), COO said on an earnings call that labour cost per order is the top priority.
- Value hypothesis: "Because the new warehouses are being staffed now, the 3PL could cut overtime in its first year by scheduling across sites from one view, worth about $1.2M a year at their published volumes."

## Cluster messaging for 1:few

| Cluster | Shared problem | Message | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expanding 3PLs | New sites, new staff | Staff new sites without overtime | Peer 3PL story |
| Cold-chain logistics | Compliance and shift rules | Compliance-safe scheduling | Audit outcome |
| E-commerce fulfilment | Seasonal peaks | Scale for peak without chaos | Peak-season numbers |
