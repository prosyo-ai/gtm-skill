# SDR onboarding plan, week by week

## Week 1: the buyer and the business
- Day 1: welcome, tools access, buddy assigned, read the ICP and persona documents.
- Days 2-3: listen to 8-10 recorded calls (good and bad); write down the buyer's words for pains.
- Day 4: product walkthrough focused on the 3 problems customers buy it for.
- Day 5: shadow a senior SDR for a full day; recap what they noticed first on each account.

## Week 2: messaging and process
- Value proposition and messaging matrix; write it back in their own words.
- Sales process, stages, qualification criteria, handoff rules.
- Write 10 first emails and 10 LinkedIn notes; manager reviews line by line.
- **Certification 1**: 30-second pitch per persona; explain the ICP and disqualifiers.

## Week 3: practice
- Daily role-plays: cold call opener, 5 objections, a discovery mini-call.
- Research and tier 30 accounts; present 3 account briefs.
- **Certification 2**: role-play pass on calls and objections; 3 emails pass review.
- First live touches to 20-30 accounts with every message reviewed.

## Weeks 4-8: ramp
| Week | New contacts per week | Review | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 50 | Every message | First positive reply |
| 5 | 75 | Sample of 10 | First meeting booked |
| 6 | 100 | Sample of 10 | 25-35% of meeting target (month 1) |
| 7-8 | 125-150 | Weekly | On track for 60-70% in month 2 |

Daily 15-minute check-in: yesterday's numbers, one thing that worked, one thing to fix.

## Weeks 9-13: own it
- Full activity and meeting targets.
- Runs one own campaign end to end (list, copy, analysis) with manager sign-off.
- Teaches one session to the next new rep.
- 90-day review: numbers, skills scorecard, development goals.

## Skills scorecard (score 1-4 at days 30, 60, 90)

| Skill | 1 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP judgement | Contacts poor fits | Rarely misses; flags bad lists |
| Research | Generic openers | Sourced, relevant hooks quickly |
| Writing | Long, about us | Short, about them, passes review first time |
| Calls | Reads script | Conversational, handles objections |
| Reply handling | Slow, generic | Same-day, books from questions |
| Self-management | Needs reminders | Plans own day, hits activity |
