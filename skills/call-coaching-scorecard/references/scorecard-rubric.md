# Call scorecard rubric

## Discovery call

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Jumps in, no agenda | States agenda | Agenda + buyer's goals asked | Agenda, buyer adds to it, permission to dig in |
| Discovery | Few or closed questions | Situation questions only | Reaches problem and some impact | Reaches impact, priority and decision process with follow-ups |
| Listening | Interrupts, talks 70%+ | Talks 55-70% | Talks 40-55%, some paraphrasing | Talks under 40%, uses the buyer's words back |
| Value | Feature tour | Generic benefits | Ties one feature to stated pain | Ties to pain + impact with matching proof |
| Objections | Argues or ignores | Answers without clarifying | Clarifies, then answers | Clarifies, answers, checks it landed |
| Next step | None or vague | "I'll send info" | Next step agreed, no date | Dated, in calendar, right people invited |

## Cold call

| Dimension | 4 looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Opener | Honest, permission-based, under 15 seconds |
| Reason | Problem stated in buyer terms with a peer reference, ends in a question |
| Engagement | Asks a follow-up question on their answer |
| Brush-off | Acknowledges, one question, respects the answer |
| Ask | Specific time options or a referral; clear outcome either way |

## Example quotes by score (discovery)

- Discovery 1: "So do you use a scheduling tool?" (closed, leads nowhere)
- Discovery 4: "You said Mondays are chaos. What happens to the rest of the week when Monday goes wrong? ... Roughly how many hours does that cost the team?"
- Listening 4: "So if I heard you right, it's not the software, it's that nobody owns cross-site cover. Is that fair?"
- Next step 2: "I'll send over some info and we can go from there."
- Next step 4: "Let's do Thursday at 10 with you and Dev; I'll send the invite now and bring the cross-site cover example."

## Focus skill picker

If several dimensions score low, coach the earliest in the call first: opening → discovery → listening → value → objections → next step. Improvements early in the call lift the later scores on their own.
