# Profile templates

## About structure

```
Line 1-2: The reader's problem, in their words. (Shows before "see more".)
Para 2:   What you have seen across many teams like theirs (pattern, insight).
Para 3:   How you help, in one or two plain sentences.
Para 4:   Proof: one or two specific results or customers (approved).
Para 5:   Low-pressure invitation + how to reach you.
```

Keep it to 120-250 words. Short paragraphs, no bullet walls.

## Example: SDR, cybersecurity, mid-market IT

> Most IT leaders at 200-1,000 person companies I talk to have the same problem: a security tool stack that grew faster than the team that runs it.
>
> I spend my week with those teams, and the pattern is consistent. Alerts pile up, nobody owns triage, and the audit is always three months away.
>
> At Acme, I help IT leaders find out where their real exposure is in a 30-minute review, before they spend a cent.
>
> Last quarter that review helped a logistics company cut their open critical findings from 140 to 12 ahead of an audit.
>
> If security debt is on your list this year, happy to compare notes. No pitch unless you ask for one.

## Example: Founder, restaurant payroll

> Running payroll for ten restaurants should not take your ops manager three days every two weeks.
>
> I started Acme after watching my family's restaurant group do exactly that: tips, split shifts and three states of tax rules, reconciled by hand.
>
> We built payroll for multi-location restaurant groups: tips, shifts and taxes handled in one run.
>
> Groups like Northwind now close payroll in under a day.
>
> If you run 5+ locations, I'd love to hear how you handle payroll today.

## Example: Agency owner, outbound for B2B SaaS

> Most B2B SaaS teams don't have a lead problem. They have a "the right accounts never hear from us at the right moment" problem.
>
> We run outbound for SaaS companies between $2M and $20M ARR: targeting, copy, sending infrastructure and reply handling.
>
> Clients like Contoso added 40 qualified meetings a quarter without hiring SDRs.
>
> If outbound is on your plan this year, send me a message and I'll share how we would approach your market.

## Headline bank

- Helping <who> <outcome> | <role> at <company>
- <Outcome> for <who> | <proof>
- I help <who> fix <problem> | ex-<credible company>
- <Who>'s go-to for <topic> | <role>
