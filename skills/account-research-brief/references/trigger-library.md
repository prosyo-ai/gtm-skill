# Trigger library

Triggers ranked by how reliably they create a buying window. "Window" is how long the urgency usually lasts.

| Trigger | Where to find it | Window | Why it matters | Example angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New leader in the buying role | Leadership posts, company news, profile changes | First 90 days | New leaders audit tools and want early wins | "Most new sales leaders I talk to review the outbound stack in month one..." |
| Funding round | Press releases, funding databases | 3-6 months | Budget plus pressure to grow fast | "With the Series B, the plan likely calls for pipeline to double..." |
| Hiring for a role your product supports | Careers page, job boards | While the listing is open | They are paying to solve the problem; you may be cheaper or faster | "Saw you're hiring three SDRs. Teams usually..." |
| Expansion: new region, office, product line | Newsroom, leadership posts | 3-6 months | New processes get built from scratch | "Opening in Germany means..." |
| Tool change | Job listings naming tools, review sites | 6 months | Migration projects open the door to adjacent tools | "Since you're moving to a new CRM..." |
| Regulation or compliance deadline | Industry news | Until the deadline | Fixed date, non-negotiable | "With the deadline in March..." |
| Bad results or layoffs | Earnings, news | 3 months | Efficiency pitches land; growth pitches do not | Lead with cost and time saved, gently |
| Competitor customer churn | Reviews, community threads | 1-3 months | Active pain with a named alternative | "Teams switching from the old way usually ask..." |
| Event participation | Event agendas, speaker lists | 2 weeks around the event | A natural, timely reason to talk | "Saw you're speaking at..." |

## Weak triggers to skip

- Generic awards and "best places to work" lists.
- Website redesigns with no strategic change.
- Anniversaries.
- Old funding (over 12 months) presented as news.

## Writing the trigger into an opener

Formula: **observation → implication → question**. Keep the observation factual and the implication humble.

- Weak: "Congrats on the funding! We help companies grow."
- Strong: "Saw the Series B last month and four SDR roles on your careers page. Teams in that spot usually hit a list-building bottleneck before they hit a headcount one. Is that on your radar yet?"
