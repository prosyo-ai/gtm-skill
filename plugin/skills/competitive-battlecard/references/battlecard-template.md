## Filled example (fictional competitor "Rotaflow")

```
WE WIN WHEN: multi-location groups (10+ sites); teams that need group-level reporting; ops leaders own the decision
WE LOSE WHEN: single-site practices; buyers want the cheapest option; IT mandates their existing suite
  → Qualify out early: "How many locations will be on it in 12 months?"

QUICK POSITIONING:
"Rotaflow is great for single offices that want something simple. Groups with 10+ locations usually pick us because they can move patients and staff across sites from one schedule."

DIFFERENTIATORS
1. Group-level schedule - fill chairs across sites - Northwind: 71% → 86% utilisation
2. Same-day reporting - decisions on today's data - live demo of the group view
3. Rollout in 3 weeks - no lost booking days - rollout plan + 2 references

LANDMINE QUESTIONS
- "How do you see open capacity across all locations today?"
- "What happens when one office is overbooked and the next one is empty?"
- "How long did rollout take for groups your size?"

OBJECTIONS
"Rotaflow is cheaper per location." → "It is. Most groups find one extra filled chair a day per site covers the difference; want to run your numbers?"
"We already use Rotaflow at two sites." → "Makes sense to start there. How do you combine their schedules with the other eight?"

DON'T: call them outdated; promise features on the roadmap; match their price without a trade
```
