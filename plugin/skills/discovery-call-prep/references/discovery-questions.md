# Discovery questions

## Situation
- Walk me through how <process> works today, from start to finish.
- Who is involved, and who owns it?
- What tools or spreadsheets does it run on?
- How many <units: reps, locations, invoices> are we talking about?

## Problem
- Where does it break or slow down?
- How often does that happen?
- What have you tried to fix it? What happened?
- If you could change one thing about it tomorrow, what would it be?

## Impact
- What does that cost you, in hours a week or month?
- What happens downstream when it goes wrong? Who notices?
- Is it affecting a target you or your team are measured on?
- Roughly what is that worth per year?

## Priority
- Why is this on your list now rather than last year?
- What happens if nothing changes in the next six months?
- Where does this rank against your other priorities this quarter?

## Decision
- If you decided to fix this, how would that decision get made?
- Who else would need to be involved or comfortable with it?
- Is there a budget cycle or timing we should know about?
- What would you need to see to be confident?
- Have you bought something like this before? How did that go?

## Follow-up prompts (use often)
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What do you mean by <their word>?"
- "Can you give me an example from last month?"
- "How do you know?"
- "And what happened then?"

## Qualification checklist

| Item | Confirmed when |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | They described it in their own words with an example |
| Impact | A number (time, money, risk) or a target it threatens |
| Priority | A reason it matters now, and what happens if nothing changes |
| Decision process | Names and steps to a signature |
| Budget timing | When money can be spent, even if not the amount |
| Champion | Someone who will push it internally |

Three or fewer confirmed: early-stage, nurture or a smaller next step. Five or six: progress to evaluation.
