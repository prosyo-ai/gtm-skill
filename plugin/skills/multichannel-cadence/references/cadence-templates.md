| 1 | LinkedIn | Connection request, note on the shared topic |

## Daily task math

Tasks per day ≈ new accounts per day × contacts per account × touches per contact ÷ cadence length in days × overlap factor (≈1.0 once running).

Example: 4 Tier 1 accounts a day × 2 contacts × 12 touches ÷ 21 days ≈ 4.6 tasks per day per new-account batch; at steady state with three weeks of overlap, about 100 tasks a day. Too many for one rep alongside calls, so drop to 2-3 new Tier 1 accounts a day.

## Cross-channel copy thread

- Email 1: "Teams your size usually lose a week a month to approvals."
- Call: "I sent you a note Tuesday about approvals eating the last week of the month. Is that true for your team?"
- LinkedIn: "Curious, does your close slow down at approvals or at reconciliation?"
- Video: 45 seconds walking through how a peer set a day-3 approval cut-off.
