# Tiering rubric

## Fit score (0-100)

| Component | Points | How to score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core ICP must-haves | 0-45 | Even split across must-haves; any miss caps fit at 49 |
| Size in the sweet spot | 0-15 | Full points in the band where win rate is highest, half in adjacent bands |
| Nice-to-haves | 0-15 | Stack match, business model detail, region |
| Reachable buying group | 0-15 | Named buyer and champion with verified channels |
| Relationship assets | 0-10 | Former customer champion now there, shared investor, warm intro path |
| Disqualifier | Fit = 0 | Overrides all |

## Timing score (0-100)

| Signal | Points | Decay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New leader in the buying role | 30 | Halves after 90 days |
| Hiring for a role your product supports | 25 | Zero when the listing closes |
| Funding round | 20 | Halves after 120 days |
| High-intent web visits (pricing, comparison, demo pages) | 25 | Halves after 14 days |
| Engagement with your content or team | 15 | Halves after 30 days |
| Third-party intent on your category | 15 | Halves after 30 days |
| Past opportunity lost to "timing" | 15 | Peaks at the date they named |

Cap timing at 100.

## Tie-breaks inside a tier

1. Higher timing first.
2. Then accounts with an existing relationship asset.
3. Then larger potential contract value.

## Calibrating the weights

After a quarter, compare tiers with outcomes:

| Tier | Accounts | Meetings | Meeting rate | Opportunities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 120 | 34 | 28% | 19 |
| 2 | 480 | 41 | 9% | 17 |
| 3 | 1,400 | 22 | 2% | 6 |

Healthy pattern: each tier converts at roughly 2-4x the tier below. If Tier 1 and Tier 2 convert at similar rates, the fit weights are not discriminating; look at which components the Tier 2 winners scored low on and raise those weights.
