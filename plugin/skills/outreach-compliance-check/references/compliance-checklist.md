# Compliance checklist

A practical pre-send checklist. Rules change and vary by country; confirm anything high-risk with counsel.

## Every region

- [ ] Sender name and company are real and visible.
- [ ] Subject line honestly reflects the content.
- [ ] Commercial nature of the message is clear.
- [ ] A simple opt-out (reply "no" or a link) in every commercial email.
- [ ] Opt-outs honoured quickly and across every mailbox, channel and future list.
- [ ] Shared suppression list checked before each send.
- [ ] Data source recorded per contact (source, date, basis).
- [ ] A ready answer to "where did you get my details?"
- [ ] A way to handle deletion and access requests.

## United States (B2B cold email)

- [ ] No misleading header information or deceptive subject lines.
- [ ] Valid physical postal address of the sender.
- [ ] Opt-out works and is honoured within the required window.
- [ ] Third parties sending on your behalf follow the same rules.

## EU and UK (business contacts)

- [ ] A documented legitimate-interest assessment: purpose, necessity, balancing test.
- [ ] Targeting is role-based and the offer is relevant to the role.
- [ ] Contact data is business data (work email), not personal or consumer data.
- [ ] Privacy information is available (link to a privacy notice) and the data source can be disclosed.
- [ ] Right to object is offered from the first message.
- [ ] Data kept only as long as needed; stale contacts removed.
- [ ] Sole traders and some partnerships may be treated as individuals: extra care.

## Canada

- [ ] Consent basis identified (express or a narrow implied-consent exception).
- [ ] Sender identification and contact information included.
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism works and is honoured promptly.

## LinkedIn and other platforms

- [ ] Platform terms followed: no prohibited automation, no fake accounts, within activity limits.
- [ ] Same opt-out respect applies: a "not interested" ends the conversation.

## Red flags: stop and ask counsel

- Consumer or personal data in a B2B campaign.
- Health, finance or children-related audiences.
- Data bought from a source that cannot document its own basis.
- Messages that would surprise the recipient about how you got their details.
