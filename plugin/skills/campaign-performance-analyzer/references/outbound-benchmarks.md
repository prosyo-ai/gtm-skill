# Outbound benchmarks

Typical ranges for well-run B2B outbound to a targeted list. They vary by market, deal size and offer; use them to spot outliers, then track your own trend.

## Cold email

| Metric | Weak | Typical | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bounce rate | > 3% | 1-2% | < 1% |
| Reply rate (any) | < 2% | 3-6% | 8%+ |
| Positive reply rate (of contacts) | < 0.5% | 1-2% | 3%+ |
| Positive share of replies | < 25% | 30-45% | 50%+ |
| Meetings per 100 contacts | < 0.5 | 0.8-1.5 | 2+ |
| Spam complaint rate | > 0.3% | < 0.1% | ~0 |

## LinkedIn

| Metric | Weak | Typical | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acceptance rate | < 20% | 25-40% | 45%+ |
| Reply rate (of accepted) | < 8% | 10-20% | 25%+ |
| Meetings per 100 invitations | < 1 | 1-3 | 4+ |

## Calls

| Metric | Typical |
| --- | --- |

## Meetings to pipeline

| Metric | Weak | Typical | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Show rate | < 70% | 75-85% | 90%+ |
| Held meeting to opportunity | < 30% | 35-50% | 60%+ |

## By step (cold email)

A healthy 4-step sequence usually gets 40-60% of its replies from step 1, 15-25% from step 2, 10-20% from step 3 and 10-15% from the break-up email. If step 1 gets almost none, the first email or deliverability is broken; if the break-up gets the most, earlier steps are too pitchy.

## Sample size guide

| Contacts per slice | How to treat results |
| --- | --- |
| < 100 | Anecdote |
| 100-300 | Directional |
| 500-1,000 | Decision-grade for big differences (2x) at typical reply rates |
| 2,000+ | Decision-grade for smaller differences (1.5x) |
