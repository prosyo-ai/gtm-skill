# ICP trait comparison and scoring

## The comparison table

Build one row per candidate trait. Use shares, not counts.

| Trait | Top 20% of customers | Lost and churned | Gap | Keep? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5-50 locations | 78% | 31% | +47 | Must-have |
| Has an operations leader | 70% | 40% | +30 | Must-have |
| Uses a modern POS | 64% | 52% | +12 | Not predictive |
| Raised funding in 12 months | 35% | 9% | +26 | Nice-to-have (timing) |
| Single location | 4% | 38% | -34 | Disqualifier |

Rules of thumb:

- Gap of 20 points or more: keep the trait.
- Gap of 10 to 20 points: nice-to-have, retest next quarter.
- Gap under 10 points: drop it, however intuitive it sounds.
- Strong negative gap: candidate disqualifier.

## Account fit score

Turn the ICP into a 100-point score so lists and inbound leads can be ranked the same way.

| Component | Points | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Must-haves met | 0-50 | Split evenly across must-haves; any miss caps the score at 49 |
| Nice-to-haves | 0-20 | Split evenly |
| Active trigger in last 90 days | 0-20 | Funding, new leader, relevant hiring, expansion |
| Reachable buyer identified | 0-10 | Named person with a verified channel |
| Disqualifier present | Score = 0 | Overrides everything |

Tiers: 80+ is Tier 1 (research and personalise), 60-79 is Tier 2 (segment-level personalization), under 60 stays out of outbound.

## Small-sample mode (under 10 customers)

1. Interview the founder about the five best and five worst conversations.
2. List the traits the best five share. Each becomes a hypothesis, not a rule.
3. Pick the two most testable hypotheses and design a 200-contact test for each.
4. After 2 to 3 weeks, keep the segment with the higher positive reply rate (a 2x gap is meaningful at this size; a 20% gap is not).

## Worked example

A payroll tool for restaurants had 34 customers. Revenue-weighted, the top seven were all groups with 8 to 30 locations and a dedicated operations manager. Single-location restaurants were 40% of logos but 70% of churn. The new ICP dropped single sites entirely, added "hiring an operations or payroll role" as the trigger and moved headcount from a filter to a check. Positive reply rate on the next 600 contacts rose from 1.1% to 3.4%.
