# Interview guide and synthesis template

## Opening (2 min)

"Thanks for making time. I'm not selling anything today; I want to understand how you made the decision, including the messy parts. Mind if I record so I can listen instead of taking notes?"

## Before (5 min)
- Walk me through how you handled <job> before.
- What was the most frustrating part? When did that last happen?
- What did it cost you: time, money, missed targets?

## Trigger (4 min)
- What happened that made you start looking for something new then, not six months earlier?
- Who first raised it?

## Search (4 min)
- What options did you consider, including doing nothing or building it yourselves?
- What did you like and dislike about each?
- What almost made you choose differently?

## Decision (4 min)
- Who was involved? Who had to say yes?
- What questions or worries came up internally?
- What made you confident enough to go ahead?

## After (4 min)
- What's different now? Can you give me a recent example?
- If it disappeared tomorrow, what would you do?
- How would you describe it to a peer?

## Follow-ups to use often
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What do you mean by <their word>?"
- "When was the last time that happened?"
- "And then what?"
