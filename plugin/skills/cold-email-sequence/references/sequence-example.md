# Annotated 4-step sequence

Segment: Heads of Finance at 200-1,000 person SaaS companies. Problem: month-end close takes too long. Proof: Contoso closes in 4 days instead of 9 (approved).

## Step 1 (day 0)

> Subject: month-end close
>
> Hi Maya, finance teams at Lumen's size usually close in 8-10 days, and most of that time goes to chasing approvals and reconciling spreadsheets.
>
> Contoso's team was at 9 days last year. They now close in 4, mostly by automating the reconciliations that used to sit with two analysts.
>
> Is a faster close on your list this year?

Job: name the problem and prove it is solvable. 60 words.

## Step 2 (day 3, same thread)

> Hi Maya, one more angle: the slow part is rarely the accounting itself. It is the 30-40 approvals that wait in inboxes over the last week of the month.
>
> If that sounds familiar, I can send the checklist Contoso used to cut them in half.

Job: a sharper insight plus a give. 48 words.

## Step 3 (day 8, new thread)

> Subject: how contoso closes in 4 days
>
> Hi Maya, a short story in case it is useful. Contoso's controller, Priya, had two analysts spending the last week of every month matching bank lines to invoices. They automated the matching, moved approvals into one queue and set a day-3 cut-off.
>
> Result: a 4-day close and one analyst moved to forecasting. Happy to share what they changed first.

Job: proof as a story; works as a standalone first email. 70 words.

## Step 4 (day 15, same thread as step 3)

> Hi Maya, I will stop here. If faster closes become a priority later, just reply "close" and I will send the Contoso checklist. Either way, good luck with quarter end.

Job: graceful exit, one-word reply option. 35 words.

## Variations by situation

| Situation | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Very senior persona (C-level) | 3 steps, each under 60 words, longer gaps (day 0, 5, 12) |
| Strong trigger (new leader, funding) | Step 1 leads with the trigger; step 2 offers a stage-specific asset |
| Technical persona | Step 2 becomes a concrete how-it-works detail, no adjectives |
| Agency or services offer | Step 3 is a mini case study with a screenshot offer (not an attachment) |
| Low volume, Tier 1 | Step 1 fully 1:1; steps 2-4 semi-personal |

## Out-of-office handling

- Return date given: pause the sequence until 2 business days after the return, then resume at the next step.
- "No longer with the company": stop, mark the contact, and look for the replacement.
- Referral in the auto-reply ("contact Sam instead"): start Sam on step 1 with a line naming the referral.
